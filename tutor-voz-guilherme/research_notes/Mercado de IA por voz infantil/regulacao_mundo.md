## 1. United States — COPPA, FTC 6(b) inquiry, state companion-chatbot laws, AI-toy moratorium, lawsuits, enforcement vs toys/edtech

### Takeaway
The US is now the most active and fragmented jurisdiction: the amended COPPA Rule has been enforceable since 22 Apr 2026 (voice recordings = personal information, separate consent for third-party disclosure, written retention/security programs), a dozen-plus states have companion-chatbot laws (CA SB 243 in force since 1 Jan 2026; NY since 5 Nov 2025; OR/WA/NE/ID/GA etc. coming 2026-2027), and California enacted a first-in-nation 4-year moratorium (2027-2031) on toys for under-16s containing an AI companion chatbot — directly relevant to an animated AI voice character for 4-8-year-olds.

### Cited Findings

**COPPA (federal) — amended Rule**
- Amended COPPA Rule published in the Federal Register 22 Apr 2025; effective 23 Jun 2025; regulated entities had until **22 Apr 2026** to comply (except § 312.11(d)(1), (d)(4) and (g), safe-harbor provisions on other dates). **[IN FORCE]** — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule); [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/ftc-publishes-final-coppa-rule-amendments)
- Audio recordings containing a child's voice are personal information; voiceprints are included in the new "biometric identifiers" definition, but the FTC dropped proposed language covering "data derived from voice data, gait data, or facial data" after comments on overbreadth. — [Hunton](https://hunton.com/privacy-and-information-security-law/ftc-publishes-final-coppa-rule-amendments); [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/ftc-publishes-updates-to-coppa-rule)
- Voice exception: operators may collect an audio file with a child's voice (and no other personal information) solely to respond to the child's request, without parental consent (originates in the FTC's 2017 enforcement policy statement; secondary sources describe it as codified/maintained — confirm exact rule text: the file must be used only for that purpose and deleted immediately after). — [Foley Hoag (Jul 2026)](https://foleyhoag.com/news-and-insights/blogs/security-privacy-and-the-law/2026/july/the-amended-children-s-online-privacy-protection-act-(coppa)-regulations-what-your-business-needs/); [BBB National Programs](https://bbbprograms.org/media/insights/blog/coppa-amended) **[verify exact scope]**
- Other key amended-Rule obligations (per law-firm summaries): separate verifiable parental consent (VPC) before disclosing children's data to third parties (e.g., targeted advertising/AI training partners) unless integral to the service; written data-retention policy (no indefinite retention); written information security program; expanded "personal information" (biometrics, government IDs); new approved VPC methods (knowledge-based questions, facial match to photo ID, "text plus"); more transparency for safe-harbor programs. — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/ftc-publishes-updates-to-coppa-rule); [Promise Legal — edtech view](https://blog.promise.legal/coppa-april-2026-amendments-edtech/)
- Civil penalties: reported at up to **$53,088 per violation (per day for continuing violations)** as of 2026 (inflation-adjusted). — [HelloWarrant blog](https://hellowarrant.com/blog/the-coppa-enforcement-clock-is-about-to-hit-zero) **[UNVERIFIED — secondary; check FTC annual inflation adjustment]**

**FTC enforcement vs connected toys / voice / kids' content**
- **Amazon Alexa (2023):** FTC/DOJ alleged Amazon kept kids' Alexa voice recordings indefinitely and undermined parents' deletion requests; **$25M civil penalty**, order to delete data and bar on using deleted/geolocation/voice data to train algorithms. — [FTC press release, May 2023](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/05/ftc-doj-charge-amazon-violating-childrens-privacy-law-keeping-kids-alexa-voice-recordings-forever-undermining-parents) (background knowledge; URL not re-fetched)
- **VTech (2018):** electronic toy maker (Kid Connect app) paid **$650,000** for collecting children's data without VPC and failing to secure it (2015 breach) — first FTC connected-toy COPPA case. — [FTC press release, Jan 2018](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2018/01/electronic-toy-maker-vtech-settles-ftc-allegations-it-violated-childrens-privacy-law-ftc-act) (background knowledge; URL not re-fetched)
- **Apitor (Sept 2025):** Chinese robot-toy maker's companion app embedded a third-party SDK (JPush) that collected children's precise geolocation without VPC; **$500,000** penalty suspended for inability to pay; order finalized 1 Oct 2025. — [Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2826/2025-09-05-ftc-coppa-enforcement-still-alive-and-well); [Sheppard Mullin/Law360](https://www.sheppard.com/insights/articles/privacy-lessons-from-ftc-settlement-with-chinese-toymaker)
- **Disney (Sept 2025):** **$10M** settlement for failing to label YouTube videos "Made for Kids", enabling data collection from children without consent. — [Nelson Mullins, 12 Sept 2025](https://www.nelsonmullins.com/insights/alerts/privacy_and_data_security_alert/all/ftc-announces-children-s-privacy-enforcements-and-launches-ai-chatbot-inquiry)

### Inferences
- An animated AI **voice** character for 4-8s in the US is squarely a "child-directed service" under COPPA: VPC before any voice/personal data collection beyond the narrow "respond-and-delete" audio exception; any use of recordings to train/improve models or sharing with an LLM vendor beyond what is "integral" likely needs separate consent and contractual controls (Apitor shows SDK/vendor leakage is enforced).
- The Amazon Alexa order (no indefinite retention; no training on data that should have been deleted) + amended Rule's written-retention requirement → default short retention of audio and transcripts, parent-visible deletion that actually works.
- California SB 867 is a potential hard blocker for a **physical toy** form factor sold in CA from 2027; an app/screen character may fall outside "toy" but would still be covered by SB 243/SB 1119-type companion rules if it offers sustained relationship-style interaction. Positioning as an *educational tutor with supervised sessions* rather than a "companion/friend" reduces exposure (several statutes and China's rules carve out educational bots) — but definitions must be checked statute by statute.
- Common US design baseline: AI disclosure (persistent and periodic), break reminders, sexual-content blocking, self-harm detection + crisis/parent escalation, no impersonation of therapists, annual reporting readiness.

- Whether the amended COPPA Rule formally codified the audio-file exception (sources conflict/ambiguous).
- FTC 6(b) outcome — no report found; any FTC AI-toy enforcement in 2026 not found.
- CA AADC Ninth Circuit status in 2026 not checked.

## 2. European Union — AI Act, GDPR art. 8, DSA minors guidelines, Toy Safety Regulation, CRA and RED

### Takeaway
The EU layers several instruments on a kids' AI voice character: AI Act prohibitions (in force since Feb 2025) and chatbot transparency (since 2 Aug 2026), with high-risk education rules delayed to Dec 2027 by the Digital Omnibus; GDPR consent age 13-16 by member state; RED cybersecurity for wireless toys since Aug 2025; CRA reporting from Sept 2026 / full Dec 2027; and the new Toy Safety Regulation (in force 1 Jan 2026, applies 1 Aug 2030) which brings digital/connected-toy risks and a digital product passport.

### Cited Findings

**EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**
- Prohibited practices (Art. 5) apply since **2 Feb 2025**, incl. AI that exploits vulnerabilities due to **age** to materially distort behavior causing significant harm (Art. 5(1)(b)) and **emotion recognition in workplaces and education institutions** (Art. 5(1)(f), except medical/safety). Penalties for prohibited practices up to **€35M or 7%** of global turnover. — [EUR-Lex, Reg. 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj); [AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/ai-act-explorer/) (background knowledge of article contents; URLs not re-fetched)
- Annex III point 3 (education and vocational training — e.g., systems determining access, evaluating learning outcomes, steering learning, monitoring tests) = high-risk. — [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) (background knowledge)
- **Digital Omnibus on AI = Regulation (EU) 2026/1744**: provisional deal 7 May 2026, EP vote 16 Jun 2026, Council 29 Jun 2026, published 24 Jul 2026, in force 27 Jul 2026. **Annex III high-risk obligations moved to 2 Dec 2027; Annex I (product-embedded, e.g., toys) to 2 Aug 2028.** — [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html); [AI Act Explorer — Digital Omnibus](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/ai-act-explorer/digital-omnibus/); [Praxikon](https://www.praxikon.com/en/posts/digital-omnibus-high-risk-postponement-december-2027)
- **Art. 50 transparency** (users must be informed they are interacting with an AI system; synthetic audio/image marking) **applies since 2 Aug 2026**, unchanged by the Omnibus; systems generating synthetic content placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until **2 Dec 2026** for Art. 50(2) marking. Commission Art. 50 guidelines adopted 20 Jul 2026. **[IN FORCE]** — [Usercentrics](https://usercentrics.com/knowledge-hub/eu-ai-act-high-risk-delay-article-50-transparency-consent/); [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html)
- Toys are listed in AI Act Annex I (Toy Safety legislation) → an AI system that is a safety component of a toy requiring third-party conformity assessment is high-risk (now from 2 Aug 2028 per Omnibus). — [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html); [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) (Annex I listing: background knowledge)

**GDPR art. 8**
- Information-society services offered directly to a child: consent valid from age 16, member states may lower to 13; below that, consent must be given/authorized by the holder of parental responsibility with "reasonable efforts" to verify. — [EUR-Lex GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) (background knowledge)
- Enforcement precedent on AI companions: Italy's Garante fined Luka Inc. (Replika) **€5M** (2025) incl. for lacking age verification; and fined OpenAI €15M (Dec 2024) incl. absence of age verification. — [Garante privacy](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/) (background knowledge; specific press releases not re-fetched) **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**

**Digital Services Act — Art. 28 minors guidelines**
- Commission published guidelines on protection of minors under DSA Art. 28 on **14 Jul 2025**: privacy/safety by default for minors' accounts, age assurance proportional to risk (verification for highest-risk content), restrictions on persuasive design/addictive features, AI-chatbot features in platforms not to be pushed on minors, etc. Applies to online platforms (user-generated content hosting/dissemination), not to a closed 1:1 app. — [European Commission — guidelines](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-guidelines-protection-minors) (background knowledge; URL not re-fetched)

**Toy Safety Regulation (EU) 2025/2509** (replaces Directive 2009/48/EC)
- Adopted 26 Nov 2025; published OJ **12 Dec 2025**; in force **1 Jan 2026**; general application **1 Aug 2030** (some articles — e.g., notified bodies — apply from 1 Jan 2026). Some secondary sources wrongly say 2026. — [EUR-Lex PDF](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32025R2509); [Bureau Veritas](https://www.cps.bureauveritas.com/newsroom/eu-toy-safety-regulation-20252509-published)
- Introduces a **Digital Product Passport** for every toy (replacing paper DoC) and extends safety assessment to digital/connected features (safety, security, privacy by design, mental health/well-being of children for connected toys). — [EaseCert](https://easecert.com/blogs/insights/eu-toy-safety-regulation-2025-2509-requirements); [Asia Quality Focus](https://www.asiaqualityfocus.com/resources/eu-toy-safety-regulation-2025-2509) (secondary; exact article on connected-toy/AI duties not verified)

**Radio Equipment Directive delegated act + Cyber Resilience Act**
- RED Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 (Art. 3(3)(d)(e)(f): network protection, personal data/privacy, fraud) applies from **1 Aug 2025** to internet-connected radio equipment and explicitly to **toys and childcare equipment** with radio functions. — [EUR-Lex, Del. Reg. 2022/30](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2022/30/oj) (background knowledge)
- Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847): vulnerability/incident reporting from **11 Sept 2026**; full essential cybersecurity requirements from **11 Dec 2027**; covers products with digital elements incl. connected toys and their apps/remote processing. — [European Commission — CRA](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act) (background knowledge)

### Inferences
- Product design must avoid anything resembling Art. 5(1)(b) "exploiting age vulnerabilities" (dark patterns, guilt-tripping the child to keep playing, manipulative persuasion to buy) and must **not infer emotions** from the child's voice in an educational context (Art. 5(1)(f) risk) — sentiment/emotion detection is a red flag in EU pitches.
- If the character evaluates learning outcomes or steers learning paths in schools, it may be Annex III high-risk from Dec 2027 (conformity assessment, risk management, human oversight, logging); a home "play-based" product with parent supervision is less exposed, but marketing to schools changes classification.
- Persistent "I'm an AI" disclosure and labeling of synthetic voice are already mandatory (Art. 50).
- A physical toy version must plan for RED (now), CRA (2027) and TSR (2030) + DPP; app-only lowers hardware burden but CRA still covers software products.

### Gaps
- Exact TSR articles on connected toys/AI and whether "psychological/mental health" risk duties apply to software; DPP technical date.
- Whether the Omnibus changed AI Act Art. 4 AI-literacy or SME relief relevant to startups (not checked).
- Italy Garante figures/dates not re-verified this session.

## 3. United Kingdom — Children's Code (AADC), Online Safety Act, ICO/Ofcom actions

### Takeaway
UK GDPR's Children's Code applies to any service likely to be accessed by under-18s and explicitly names connected toys; the Online Safety Act child-safety duties (since 25 Jul 2025) cover chatbots only partially (user-to-user/search/porn), a gap the government said it will close; Ofcom has begun investigating an AI companion service over age checks.

### Cited Findings
- **Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code)**: statutory code, 15 standards (best interests, DPIA, age-appropriate application, high-privacy defaults, data minimisation, no nudge techniques, geolocation off, parental controls transparent to the child, profiling off by default, **connected toys and devices** must comply). In force since 2 Sept 2021. — [ICO — Introduction to the Children's code](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/introduction-to-the-childrens-code/); [Eversheds Sutherland](https://www.eversheds-sutherland.com/en/united-kingdom/insights/icos-childrens-code-applies-from-today-what-you-need-to-know)
- ICO guidance: firms should ensure accuracy, avoid bias and explain AI-based profiling of children. — [ICO — recommended actions](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/best-interests-self-assessment/step-4-prioritise-actions/recommended-actions-in-the-children-s-code/)
- ICO 2026 priorities include AI, biometrics, children and enforcement. — [Bratby Law](https://bratby.law/ico-2026-priorities-ai-biometrics-children-enforcement/). Precedents (background knowledge): TikTok fined £12.7M (Apr 2023) for processing under-13s' data without consent; ICO preliminary enforcement notice to Snap over "My AI" risk assessment (Oct 2023), closed after Snap remediated (2024). **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**
- **Online Safety Act**: Ofcom's Protection of Children Codes / child-safety duties enforceable from **25 Jul 2025** (highly effective age assurance for porn and harmful content). Penalties up to £18M or 10% of qualifying worldwide revenue. (background knowledge) — see [Ofcom](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/investigation-into-x-and-scope-of-the-online-safety-act)
- Ofcom (Feb 2026): AI content is in scope only if user-generated or search content; **standalone chatbots limited to user-chatbot exchanges, without search and unable to generate porn, may fall outside the OSA**. Government said it would bring AI chatbot providers into scope of illegal content duties; House of Lords approved an amendment creating criminal penalties for chatbot operators failing to protect children (enactment not confirmed). **[PENDING]** — [Ofcom update on X and OSA scope](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/investigation-into-x-and-scope-of-the-online-safety-act); [Lewis Silkin (Feb 2026)](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/02/23/online-safety-reforms-to-be-fast-tracked-amid-rising-ai-risks-102mk2r)

## 4. China — Minors Protection Law, "minor mode", generative AI rules, anthropomorphic AI measures, AI toy boom

### Takeaway
China regulates hardest and most specifically: since 15 Jul 2026 the CAC's Interim Measures on Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services ban virtual partner/relative services for minors, require guardian consent for under-14s, a minor mode with time limits, AI disclosure and 2-hour usage prompts — while the state simultaneously promotes a fast-growing AI-toy industry (~¥29bn in 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services (CAC Order No. 21)**: draft Dec 2025; final issued by CAC + four agencies **10 Apr 2026**; **effective 15 Jul 2026**. ## 5. Other jurisdictions — Australia, South Korea, India (and notes)

### Takeaway
Australia has the most directly applicable rules for AI companions (eSafety Age-Restricted Material Codes covering AI companion chatbots since 9 Mar 2026, plus the under-16 social-media minimum age from Dec 2025); South Korea's AI Basic Act (in force 22 Jan 2026) mandates generative-AI notice/labeling with a ≥1-year fine grace period; India's DPDP Rules require verifiable parental consent for all under-18s from May 2027.

### Cited Findings
**Australia**
- Social Media Minimum Age (under-16 ban) applies from **10 Dec 2025** to age-restricted social media platforms (fines up to A$49.5M) — scope is social media, not 1:1 kids' learning apps. (background knowledge) — see [eSafety](https://www.esafety.gov.au/)
- **Age-Restricted Material Codes** in effect **9 Mar 2026**: cover AI companion chatbots — must prevent sexually explicit conversations with children and conversations encouraging self-harm/suicide, with age assurance; eSafety enforces against systemic non-compliance; max civil penalties reported **A$49.5M**. — [eSafety fact sheet (Mar 2026)](https://www.esafety.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/Age-Restricted-Material-Codes-fact-sheet-March2026.pdf); [AI Geek](https://aigeek.au/news/esafety-age-restricted-codes/)
- eSafety transparency notices (Oct 2025; report 24 Mar 2026) to Character.AI, Nomi, Chai, Chub AI: weak age verification; three of four did not refer users expressing suicidal ideation to support; Chai moved companions behind paid subscription, Nomi committed to more age assurance. — [eSafety findings](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/basic-online-safety-expectations/ai-services/findings-october-2025); [Echo](https://www.echo.net.au/2026/03/ai-companions-are-putting-children-at-risk-says-esafety-report/)
- No public penalty under the codes found as of Nov 2026. — [eSafety fact sheet](https://www.esafety.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/Age-Restricted-Material-Codes-fact-sheet-March2026.pdf)

**South Korea**
- **AI Basic Act + Enforcement Decree in force 22 Jan 2026** (first comprehensive national AI law in force in Asia): obligations on transparency (notify users of generative AI, label/watermark outputs), safety for high-impact AI, local representative for foreign firms. MSIT grace period of **at least one year** before fines (fines capped at **₩30M ≈ US$20k**); corrective orders prioritized. — [Cooley (27 Jan 2026)](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-01-27-south-koreas-ai-basic-act-overview-and-key-takeaways); [trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/south-korea-ai-basic-act); [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260129/unclear-guidance-vague-terms-in-ai-basic-act-leave-businesses-in-limbo)
- Korea's PIPA requires legal-guardian consent for processing data of children under 14. (background knowledge) **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**

**India**
- **DPDP Rules, 2025** notified **13 Nov 2025** (G.S.R. 846(E)); child = under 18; Rule 10 requires verifiable parental consent and checks that the parent is an identifiable adult (e.g., via existing reliable identity data or DigiLocker-type tokens — sources disagree whether a method is mandated); Rule 10 applies from **13 May 2027** (18-month phase-in); penalties up to **₹200 crore** for child-data breaches of duty. Section 9 also bars tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children. **[PENDING — May 2027]** — [Wikipedia — DPDP Rules 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Rules,_2025); [dcomply — rules and dates](https://dpdpa.dcomply.in/rules/); [Consently](https://www.consently.in/blog/verifiable-parental-consent-dpdp-section-9)

### Inferences
- Australia is the closest analogue to the US state laws for chatbots (sexual content and self-harm safeguards + age assurance) and has an active regulator using transparency notices — a likely early-scrutiny market.
- India's "no behavioural monitoring of children" (s.9) is in tension with learning analytics/parent dashboards; would need an exemption or careful framing (educational exemptions are provided in the Rules' schedules for some institutions — not verified).

## 7. Cross-jurisdiction design implications for an animated AI voice character for ages 4-8

### Takeaway
The global convergence is clear: verifiable parental consent before voice capture, minimal/short audio retention, no training on kids' data without separate consent, persistent AI disclosure, no romantic/family/therapist role-play, self-harm and sexual-content safeguards with escalation to parents, time limits/break prompts, no engagement-maximizing dark patterns, no emotion recognition (EU), and positioning as supervised educational play rather than "companion" to stay outside the strictest companion/toy regimes (CA SB 867, China Order 21, state companion laws).

### Cited Findings
- Voice = personal information under COPPA; VPC; written retention/security programs (US, since 22 Apr 2026). — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule)
- Regular AI disclosure + 3-hour break reminders for minors + sexual-content blocking + suicide protocol (CA SB 243; NY Art. 47; OR, WA). — [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/04/2026-State-Chatbot-Laws-Key-Provisions-and-Regulatory-Trends)
- Toys with AI companion chatbots for under-16s banned for sale in CA 2027-2031. — [CA Senate SD18](https://sd18.senate.ca.gov/news/first-nation-ai-toy-moratorium-signed-law)
- EU: AI disclosure (Art. 50, since 2 Aug 2026); bans on exploiting age vulnerabilities and on emotion recognition in education (since Feb 2025). — [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html); [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)
- China: no virtual partner/relative for minors, guardian consent under 14, minor mode with time limits, 2-hour prompts, anti-retention tactics ban (since 15 Jul 2026); educational tutoring carve-out. — [AI Safety China](https://aisafetychina.substack.com/p/china-bans-ai-partners-for-minors)
- Australia: AI companions must prevent sexual and self-harm conversations with children and use age assurance (since 9 Mar 2026). — [eSafety fact sheet](https://www.esafety.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/Age-Restricted-Material-Codes-fact-sheet-March2026.pdf)
- UK: high-privacy defaults, no nudges, profiling off, connected-toy compliance, transparency to the child about parental monitoring (Children's Code). — [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/introduction-to-the-childrens-code/)

### Inferences
- Concrete checklist for the product/pitch: (1) parent-first onboarding with VPC (adult verification) before mic access; (2) on-device or ephemeral audio, transcripts retained only as long as needed, parent deletion in dashboard; (3) no third-party SDKs/trackers in the kids' experience (Apitor lesson); (4) LLM vendor contracts with zero-retention/no-training; (5) character says it's an AI, never claims feelings/being human or a family member/friend substitute; (6) session timers and break prompts (≤ ~20-40 min for 4-8 aligns with China's minor-mode tiers and US 3h rule as an upper bound); (7) content filters + escalation (child distress → gentle redirect + parent alert, no therapist role); (8) no emotion recognition from voice; (9) no loot-box/streak pressure or in-character upselling; (10) dashboard transparent to child ("your grown-up can see what we did").
- Form factor: app/screen-first avoids toy-law exposure (CA SB 867, EU TSR/RED/CRA hardware duties); a plush/hardware version should wait for clarity on SB 867 definitions.
- Positioning as "supervised educational play / English tutor" rather than "AI companion/friend" may move the product outside several companion-chatbot definitions — but verify each statute's carve-outs (China explicitly carves out tutoring; US state definitions vary).

### Gaps
- No jurisdiction-specific legal opinion obtained; statute-level definitions of "companion chatbot" vs "educational tool" were not compared text-by-text.
- Time budget prevented coverage of Canada, Japan, Singapore, UAE and Latin America ex-Brazil.
